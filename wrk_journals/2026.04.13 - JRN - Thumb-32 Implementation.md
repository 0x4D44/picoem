### Thumb-32 (~170 encodings)
Complete:
- Data processing: modified immediate (10 ops), plain binary (MOVW/MOVT/
  ADDW/SUBW/ADR/BFI/BFC/UBFX/SBFX/SSAT/USAT), shifted register (10 ops)
- Data processing register: CLZ, RBIT, REV.W, REV16.W, REVSH.W, wide shifts,
  wide extends, parallel add/sub (24 ops), QADD/QDADD/QSUB/QDSUB, SEL
- Load/store: single (all sizes, all addressing modes, PLD guard),
  multiple (STMIA/DB, LDMIA/DB), dual (LDRD/STRD), exclusive (LDREX/STREX)
- Multiply: MUL/MLA/MLS, SMULL/UMULL/SMLAL/UMLAL, SDIV/UDIV, all DSP
  multiply variants (halfword, dual, word×half, MSW, USAD8, UMAAL)
- Branches: B.W T3/T4, BL, TBB/TBH
- System: MSR/MRS (all SYSm), SP banking, hints, DSB/DMB/ISB/CLREX
- IT blocks: full state machine, flag suppression, CMP/CMN/TST exemption
- FPU: VADD/VSUB/VMUL/VDIV/VNEG/VABS/VSQRT, VMLA/VMLS/VNMLA/VNMLS,
  VFMA/VFMS/VFNMA/VFNMS, VCMP/VCMPE, VCVT (F32↔S32/U32), VMOV,
  VMRS/VMSR, VLDR/VSTR/VLDM/VSTM/VPUSH/VPOP

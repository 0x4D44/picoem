## Stage 1 — Design (HLD)

Dispatched design agent. First draft (v1.0.0) had two issues I pushed
back on:

1. **§A proposed a full per-core serial drain** (mdrp2350-style).
   This would have silently broken bank-contention: `core0_bank_touched`
   is OR-accumulated per-pair and wiped by `end_core1_step()`, so
   serial drain would turn every core-1 SRAM access into a union-
   contention false-positive. The main HLD's interleaving claim was
   correct; reversing it was wrong.
2. **§D proposed three copies of a one-line helper** — not real
   deduplication. Pushed back with two alternatives.

Design agent revised to v1.1.0:

- §A: **targeted fix** (relax guard, arm both cores independently,
  advance clock by `max(c0, c1)`). Preserves per-instruction
  interleaving and per-pair contention. ~15 lines.
- §B: four contract tests in new `mod quantum_contract` in
  `src/tests.rs`. Contract #3 uses `pad_out` observable (idiomatic
  for this codebase; `StateMachine::pc` is `pub(crate)`, not
  suitable).
- §D: **don't factor** (12 localised sites, builder call is
  declarative and immediately greppable; `single_cycle_emu()` would
  hide quantum-1 intent).
- §C: `tech_debt.md` entry drafted.

**Surprise finding (v1.1.0 §A analysis):** mdrp2350 has no live
contention model — `arbitrate_pair`/`arbitrate_stall` are defined but
not called from `read32`/`write32` hot-path. mdrp2040's live model is
unique to this chip. Separate tech-debt candidate (cross-core
contention test coverage), not blocking.

HLD signed off at v1.1.0. Commit 0ef90d2+ remains unamended.

Status: **design complete. Moving to Stage 2.**

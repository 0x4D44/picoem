## 7. Recommendation

**PROCEED to Stage 1b.**

Caveats for Stage 1b:

1. **HLD §4.1 text is stale.** Update the "Stale integration with main"
   paragraph at `HLD V1 §4.1` to reflect that field-access drift is
   resolved. The wording misled the estimate by 2× in the other direction.
2. **Don't rip out the `CoreBus` generic.** The HLD §6.2 item 1 said
   "generic bound removed; workers invoke it with a concrete `&mut Bus`
   view". That's wrong for the current tree — both `Bus` and `WorkerBus`
   are `CoreBus` implementors, and removing the generic would require
   rewriting all of `core::execute*`, `decode.rs`, `exceptions.rs`,
   `coprocessor.rs`, `execute_fpu.rs`. Keep the generic. Workers pass
   `&mut bus` where `bus: WorkerBus` — it already works.
3. **The `paced_bench_rp2350` + `onerom_cpu_speed_grade_rp2350` binaries
   also import `mdrp2350::threaded::*` directly** — not just the two
   binaries the HLD lists. Stage 1b's grep success criterion ("No
   `use mdrp2350::threaded::*` in any harness binary") will fail unless
   those two are also migrated, or the criterion is relaxed to
   "no harness uses `ThreadedEmulator` directly; re-exports of
   `SpinBarrier` / `RunTimings` are OK for bench instrumentation".
   Recommend the latter — the paced-bench timing printer is already
   consuming `RunTimings` internals, which is appropriate for a
   benchmarking tool.
4. **`Emulator::bus()` accessor does not exist** on `Emulator` today and
   is not called by any harness. HLD §5.4 / §8 risk 8 auditing work is a
   no-op for RP2350. (RP2040 still to check in Stage 3.)
5. **Panic containment (§5.5) is ~70% implemented.** `BarrierResult::Poisoned`,
   `poisoned: bool` on `ThreadedEmulator`, `catch_unwind` wrappers at
   three call sites, and `poison_breaks_waiters` test already exist. The
   missing piece is the public `EmulatorError::WorkerPanicked { which,
   message }` error type and the "one-shot after panic" guarantee. Don't
   rewrite the scaffolding.

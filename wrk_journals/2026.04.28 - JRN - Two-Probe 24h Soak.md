## New script: `fuzz-runs/run-test-silicon.sh`

Mirrors the structure of `run-probe.sh` but wraps a single `test_silicon --soak <remaining>s` invocation rather than a `--fuzz N` batch loop. The orchestrator handles its own case shuffling, watchdog, and probe reattach; the shell wrapper only adds defense-in-depth restart-until-deadline logic in case the orchestrator panics outright. 30s back-off between restart attempts.

## What's been learned

- `probe-rs info` returns rc=0 even on probe-open failure — surprising, makes scripted health checks brittle. Documented in the watcher script's comments. Worth raising upstream or in our own runbook.
- The WinUSB wedge that struck both probes simultaneously on first attempt suggests the wedge predates our session — likely left over from a session that ended uncleanly earlier today (the four-track infrastructure push journal references "probes are running fuzz" but the latest probe.log mtime was 2026-04-26, so that journal entry was aspirational, not realised).

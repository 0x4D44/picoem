### Stage 2 — Hardware bank-conflict verification
**Bank-conflict theory is WRONG.**

Ran targeted tests on real RP2354 via bank_conflict_test binary.
The +1 cycle is NOT caused by I-bus/D-bus same-bank conflict.

Actual finding: **banks 2 and 6 always show +1 cycle (CYCCNT=7)**,
regardless of whether fetch and data are in the same or different banks.

| Bank | CYCCNT | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| 0 | 6 | normal |
| 1 | 6 | normal |
| 2 | **7** | +1 stall |
| 3 | 6 | normal |
| 4 | 6 | normal |
| 5 | 6 | normal |
| 6 | **7** | +1 stall |
| 7 | 6 | normal |

Banks 2 and 6 share bit pattern `(word_addr & 7) == 2 or 6` — bit 2 set,
bit 0 clear. This is a hardware characteristic of the RP2350 SRAM fabric,
not bus arbitration. Possibly related to SRAM bank addressing hardware.

**Implication for Stage 3:** Don't build I-bus/D-bus conflict model.
Instead, model the bank 2/6 extra wait state as a per-bank property.

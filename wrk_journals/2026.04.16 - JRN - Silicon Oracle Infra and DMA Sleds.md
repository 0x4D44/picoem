### B1 — `test_silicon` threading fix (completed, 2026-04-16 ~20:20 UTC)

**Root cause:** Windows WinUSB/nusb USB handles are thread-affine. probe-rs's `Session` is marked `Send` at the Rust type level, but moving it into a worker thread via `thread::spawn` crosses the OS-level thread affinity boundary, causing `session.core(0)` to fail at runtime. Standalone oracles don't hit this because they keep Session on main thread throughout.

**Fix:** removed the worker-thread + watchdog model. `run_oracle_against` wrapper and `WorkerResult` deleted. `run_one_oracle` now called directly with `&mut session` on the calling thread in both `single_pass` and `soak_loop`. ~90 lines removed, ~15 added.

**Tradeoff:** per-oracle 60s watchdog gone. probe-rs has its own USB-level timeouts; wedge recovery in soak runs may require a signal-handler approach later if it becomes an issue.

**Collateral work:** B1 also corrected two array-size typos in `silicon_scenarios.rs` (lines 1677 `[u16; 48]`→`[u16; 64]` and 1809 `[u16; 48]`→`[u16; 49]`) that were blocking compilation. These looked like in-flight C1 work — **will need to verify C1 doesn't stomp them**.

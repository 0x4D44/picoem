## Course correction (verification finding)

While verifying call sites, I discovered the brief misattributed the
callers for item (b). There are **two** `shared()` methods in the
threaded subtree:

- `WorkerBus::shared()` at `crates/mdrp2040/src/threaded/bus.rs:152` —
  `pub(crate)`, the actual brief target. **Zero callers anywhere**
  (production or test).
- `ThreadedEmulator::shared()` at `crates/mdrp2040/src/threaded/emulator.rs:331`
  and the corresponding `crates/mdrp2350/src/threaded/emulator.rs:430` —
  `pub`, **load-bearing**. Callers:
  - `mdrp2350/src/lib.rs:958-964`
  - `mdrp2350/src/threaded/emulator.rs:2681`
  - `harness/src/bin/onerom_cpu_speed_grade_rp2350.rs:871,891`

The three test sites the brief flagged (`:952`, `:953`, `:1372`) call
`threaded.shared()` — that resolves to the **public**
`ThreadedEmulator::shared()`, not the private `WorkerBus::shared()`.

### Implications

1. `WorkerBus::shared()` is in fact pure zero-caller dead code (~4
   lines). Trivial delete.
2. The `shared_accessor_returns_state` test at `emulator.rs:947-954`
   is still a worthless self-test — it asserts
   `Arc::ptr_eq(x, &Arc::clone(x))` which is a tautology guaranteed by
   `Arc::clone` semantics. Delete it (the brief's instinct was right
   even if the attribution was off).
3. The `drop_without_running_quanta` test at `emulator.rs:1367-1382`
   IS testing real Drop behaviour (Arc strong-count drops on
   `ThreadedEmulator::drop`). It uses the load-bearing public accessor
   as its probe; no rewrite needed. **Keep as-is.**

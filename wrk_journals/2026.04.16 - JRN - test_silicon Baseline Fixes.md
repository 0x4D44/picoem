- `--probe` flag works correctly (banner shows selector). However, `test_silicon` orchestrator still crashes at `session.core(0)` in worker thread — the root cause is cross-thread session transfer, not probe selection. The `--probe` fix is correct but doesn't resolve this second independent bug. Standalone oracles used for validation.
- `adc_one_shot` still crashes probe even with pad configuration. The ADC analog subsystem appears fundamentally hostile to halted-core probe access. Recommend gating behind `--include-adc` or moving to red-path catalogue.

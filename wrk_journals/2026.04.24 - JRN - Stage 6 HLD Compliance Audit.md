## Observations (non-gap)

1. **Implementation detail divergence — not a gap.** HLD §5.2 sketched an `enum Inner { Serial(SerialState), Threaded(ThreadedEmulator) }` field. Actual implementation uses a flat `execution_model: ExecutionModel` enum plus `threaded: Option<ThreadedEmulator>` with match-dispatch in `run`/`run_quantum`. Same semantic (static dispatch, no trait object, no allocation); shape chosen for integration with the pre-existing builder. HLD's "This is a clarity argument, not a perf argument" phrasing supports the present form.
2. **Test count: 19 + 18 = 37 curated** — at the lower end of HLD's ~20–30-per-chip target but inside the specified range.
3. **SpinBarrier watchdog overhead 6% vs HLD target <2%** — documented in `tech_debt.md:17-23`, Stage 5 accepted as correctness-over-perf trade-off.

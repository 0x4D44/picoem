### Rationale for `--exclude adc`

Unchanged from 04-19: `adc_one_shot` crashes probe-rs with "An ARM specific
error" before any register compare (tech_debt.md:1218). Shuffle-ordering under
soak means the crash takes out the rest of the iteration's peripherals,
reducing effective throughput. `--exclude adc` substring covers both
`adc_one_shot` and `adc_round_robin_2ch`; no other scenario names match.

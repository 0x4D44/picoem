## Progress

### Research complete

Key findings:
1. Phase 2 is largely built — contention, banking, APB, atomics all exist
2. RP2350 has 4 upstream masters (not 6) — I-bus/D-bus merged per processor
3. 17 downstream ports (we have 15 — missing XIP_MAIN1, SIOB split)
4. I-bus/D-bus +1 cycle may be internal M33 multiplexer, not fabric
5. BUS_PRIORITY at 0x40064000, configurable priority per master
6. Contention model has single-slot limitation (only tracks last port)

### Devil's advocate review

Key challenges:
1. Multi-access instructions (LDM/STM) only track last port — contention
   model incomplete for multi-word operations
2. Bank conflict theory is unverified — don't build 4 stages around it
   until hardware data confirms it
3. Exit condition "fewer mismatches" is not concrete — need pass/fail criteria
